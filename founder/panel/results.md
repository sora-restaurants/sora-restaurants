## What would flip a no

- A free trial running in a restaurant I already know, with the numbers showing more covers or fewer mistakes before I pay anything.
- A working pilot in a restaurant like mine, with a named owner I can phone, showing that tables turn faster and the kitchen screen does not drop orders, ideally with a free trial month.
- A direct link to my POS so orders are not keyed in twice, plus a restaurant my size already running it for a few months that I can call and ask.
- A free trial running in my branch for a month, with the numbers showing more orders and fewer walk-outs.
- A live reference restaurant like mine showing a month of real numbers, with setup and staff training done for me and a pilot I can stop without a penalty.
- A free pilot in my own restaurant, running through a load-shedding evening, with proof that the tablets keep working and that my older customers still get a waiter if they ask for one.
- A working direct link to my POS, or two or three live restaurants of my kind that show orders going straight to the kitchen screen without double entry.
- Two or three sit-down restaurants like mine running it live for a few months, with owners I can phone and ask about breakdowns and staff pushback.
- A proven live pilot in a sit-down restaurant like mine, with the QR orders flowing into my POS without double entry, and my regulars over 45 actually ordering on their phones.
- Two or three existing pilot restaurants that I can call, showing fewer order errors or faster table turns, plus a free or cancellable trial period on my busiest nights.
- A direct link to my POS that works, shown running in a restaurant like mine that I can call and ask about.
- A working restaurant like mine that I can call and visit, showing fewer walk-outs or faster table turns, with the setup done on site and a free trial month before the first charge.

# Sora pricing

## The price (decided 2026-11-26)

**R1,200 a month per restaurant**, flat, before VAT. No commission on orders. Setup and on-site staff training are included.

- **Margin:** variable cost is about R190 per restaurant a month, so each one leaves **R1,010 (84%)**. Setup costs about R800 to R1,000 once (QR stands, travel), which is earned back in the first month.
- **Break-even:** without founder pay, **4 restaurants** (month 5 of the plan). With 3 founder salaries, **80** (CFO tool, `numbers.json`, `numbers-unpaid.json`).
- **Competitors:** no SA vendor publishes a flat rand price for QR table ordering. Pilot POS charges R1,100 to R2,800 per terminal a month, but for a full POS (`competitors.md`).

## The risk

The simulated buyer panel's acceptable range was **R250 to R899**. 18 of the 20 buyers called R1,200 too expensive (`pricing-curve.md`). These are simulated buyers, not proof, but they say what to expect: R1,200 needs proof and the POS link to back it up.

If sign-ups come in at half the plan, year 1 loses about R13,150 without founder pay and needs about R44,690 of cash. That is still survivable.

## Opening offer

The website already promises founding restaurants "a reduced rate ... agreed up front". Suggested rate: **R899 a month for the first 6 months, then R1,200**, agreed in writing, in return for permission to use their results as a case study. R899 is the top of the panel's acceptable range and still leaves R709 a month. It is a real introductory rate that ends on a date, not a fake "was" price.

## What to watch

Record the reaction to the price in every pitch. If 3 or more serious prospects in a row turn it down on price alone, test a lower entry price (for example R899) before cutting the headline price.

## Top price objections (panel, simulated)

1. "You are not live anywhere yet, so I would be the guinea pig." They want proof before they pay.
2. "If my staff have to ring everything up on the POS anyway, why pay for a screen and a QR code?" The POS link matters more than the price.
3. They want a free trial in a restaurant they know, with the numbers to show it works.

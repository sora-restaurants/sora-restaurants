## 2. Price range (comparable item)

Only 3 public prices found for ordering products: Mr Yum 4.9% commission (2020), Serviro EUR 9/month, Qroda USD 8 to 20/month. **No public ZAR flat monthly price for QR table ordering was found**, so lowest / median / highest in rand cannot be computed honestly. Context: Pilot POS R1,100 to R2,800 per terminal per month is a full POS, not a like-for-like. Sora's R299 / R699 / R1,299 is unbenchmarked in rand; foreign flat prices (about EUR 9 to USD 20) suggest the low end is cheap but those lack waiter calls/kitchen screens. 10 of 10 vendor rows had either no or non-comparable public ZAR price.

## 3. Positioning map (text)

Axes: cost model (commission vs flat fee) and fit (replaces POS vs works alongside POS).
- Commission + alongside POS: Mr Yum (4.9%, links to POS).
- Flat fee + alongside POS: Deonde (claims R0 commission, flat subscription; POS link unverified), Serviro/Qroda/Tablewise (global), **Sora (target)**.
- Flat/per-terminal + replaces POS: Pilot, Lightspeed, Yoco ecosystem.
- Payments-led: Zapper (QR pay), no ordering.
Flat fee + works alongside a named SA POS is not clearly occupied in what I could verify.

## 5. The gap

Weak evidence only. The one pattern: the only SA-evidenced QR ordering vendor with public pricing took a percentage (Mr Yum 4.9%), the flat-fee SA claimant (Deonde) hides its price, and global flat-fee tools have no SA proof. A flat, public-ZAR-price, in-person-trained QR ordering product that sits alongside a named SA POS (e.g. Pilot) may be open. Not proven: I did not confirm Pilot lacks its own QR ordering, and Mr Yum's current SA status is unknown. If Pilot's Webbieshop QR claim holds, the gap for Pilot users is smaller.

## 6. The threat

Fastest copy: Pilot (already has waiter ordering and a reported QR integration; could bundle QR for free in the POS). Next: Deonde (already flat-fee, SA payments, QR table option). Third: a payment/POS vendor (Yoco, Zapper) adding a free ordering layer to the card machine they already sell.

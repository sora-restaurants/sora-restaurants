# Sora

- What it is: a subscription web platform that gives South African restaurants a branded QR-code menu customers order from at the table, a live kitchen screen, waiter calls, and an owner dashboard, without replacing the restaurant's POS.
- Who it is for: independent and small-chain sit-down restaurants in South Africa (braai spots, cafés, wine-farm restaurants, franchise branches such as a single Spur outlet) whose owners want faster table ordering without new hardware or a POS migration.
- What it sells, at what price (founders' hypotheses, not yet validated): Starter R299/month (branded page, menu, hours, contact, delivery links, training); Pro R699/month (adds QR ordering, waiter calls, kitchen dashboard, order status); Premium R1,299/month (adds advanced analytics and eligible POS integrations/support). All plans include staff training. No per-transaction commission. Payment processing, POS licences, domains, SMS/WhatsApp and printer hardware are extra.
- Where and how: South Africa; sold directly by a founder who handles sales and staff training; delivered as a web app (no customer app download); runs on the restaurant's existing phones/tablets.
- Budget and constraints: three founders (two developers, one sales/operations); budget not stated; building on free tiers until a pilot; payments, POS integration and printing deliberately deferred; POPIA applies.

## Recording tips

- Record each line as its own take, then line them up in CapCut over the video. That is easier than one perfect 60-second take.
- A quiet room and a phone held about a hand's width from the mouth is enough.
- If a line runs long, drop the extra words rather than speeding up: "Need a waiter? One tap." still works.
- Keep the music low under the voice.

# Sora reel: storyboard

Format of the reference reel (The Creative Dudes, "Part 3"), rebuilt for Sora with Sora's own story.
Their script, art, logo and audio are not copied; only the structure is.

- 9:16 vertical, 1080 x 1920, about 60 seconds (theirs runs 91).
- One world throughout: a night-time isometric 3D miniature restaurant (brick wall, bar counter, wooden floor, warm
  table lamps, little people at tables), seen from above at 45 degrees. The camera pushes in and pulls back.
- Captions: white bold sans, bottom third, two lines. The second line is in the accent colour and italic.
  Sora version: white plus Sora gold (#d9952b) on the dark scene.
- A small pill label top centre names the chapter, like "SORA · QR ORDERING".
- UI appears as floating phone and tablet screens over the scene, with a hand-cursor tapping.
- Upbeat electronic background track with a soft "pop" on every new element. Optional voice-over reading the captions.

## Honesty rules (Sora has no live restaurants yet)

The reference's middle act fakes nothing for them, because their comments are real. For Sora, do not show
"our comments exploded", invented comment bubbles, made-up restaurant names as customers, prices or tier cards.
Use the questions owners really asked (POS, Wi-Fi, older guests) as the bubbles instead, labelled as questions.

## End action

"Comment DEMO" only works if someone replies to every comment by hand or a DM auto-reply tool (ManyChat or similar)
is set up. Until then, "Link in bio" to the waitlist is the honest call.

## Prompts for an AI video tool (Higgsfield or similar), one per shot

Shared style line, add to every prompt: "isometric 3D miniature diorama, cosy restaurant at night, brick wall, warm
table lamps, wooden floor, small stylised people, soft studio lighting, tilt-shift depth of field, dark navy
background, clean, no text, no logos, vertical 9:16".

1. "Wide shot of a busy restaurant diorama, one waiter hurrying between tables, three guests raising hands, slow camera push in."
2. "Close on a two-person table, a guest holds a smartphone over a small QR code card on the table, camera moves in over their shoulder."
3. "Kitchen pass of the restaurant diorama, a wall-mounted tablet glowing, a chef in white reaching up to tap it, slow orbit."
4. "Same restaurant, calm and tidy, every table with a soft glowing ring on the floor around it, slow pull back to the whole room."
5. "A restaurant owner relaxing on a couch at home at night, looking at a phone, warm lamp light, isometric miniature style."

Generate the scenes without text or UI, then lay the Sora screens and captions on top in the editor (CapCut, Canva or
Premiere): AI tools draw readable UI and words badly, and the screens should be the real product.
Take the phone, kitchen and dashboard screens from the live demo or the website's mockups as screen recordings.

# Meeting brief: Andile and Ayanda Ngidi, La Vie Hospitality Group

Business information from public sources only. Unconfirmed items are marked.

## Who they are

- Twin brothers from Durban. They worked as waiters and bartenders while studying business, then ran an events company in their early 20s, then had a short stint in corporate. (Get It, Apr 2024)
- They co-founded **The Danté Experience** in Umhlanga: three storeys, a restaurant, five bars and a rooftop. They **sold it in 2022**. (Get It)
- They moved to Ballito and opened three venues side by side at the **New Salt Rock Centre**, Salt Rock, as a hub with "something for everyone". (Get It; Independent on Saturday, Apr 2024)
- **Roles:** Ayanda handles the numbers and admin, Andile is the people person. (Get It) → Show Ayanda the owner dashboard and costs; show Andile the guest and staff experience.
- Their concept came from hospitality they saw on trips to the US. (Get It)

## The venues (La Vie Hospitality Group)

| Venue | What it is | What's known |
|---|---|---|
| **Donna Modern Italian** | Family Italian, kids' area | Bookings on Dineplan (4.4 from 279 reviews, service 4.4). Delivery on **Uber Eats**. Takes part in Restaurant Week. One review said the waiter didn't know the dishes; one blamed poor management. |
| **Butcher & Wine** | Smart steakhouse, also seafood and sushi, wall of wine | Dineplan 4.5. Tuesday to Sunday, lunch and dinner. A 2024 weekday lunch special was R135 (unconfirmed now). |
| **Saint Yves** | Cocktail bar for after-work drinks and weekends | Listed on Crave with no reviews yet. Nothing public on service. |
| **Kung Thai Salt Rock** | **A franchise** of the Durban North Thai restaurant, run by the twins as part of La Vie (Time Out, Feb 2026) | Shop 2B, Tuesday to Sunday, about 12:00 to 21:00. |

## What this means for the pitch

1. **They're operators and dealmakers.** They've built and sold a venue, and they run four. Talk margins, table turns, labour and walk-outs, not features.
2. **A group, not a restaurant.** One Sora account across four venues, each with its own menu, kitchen screen and dashboard. Ask for a single-venue start, then a group rollout.
3. **Start at Saint Yves or Donna.** At Saint Yves: drinks orders and pay-first at a busy bar. At Donna: parents ordering and calling a waiter without leaving the kids. Keep Butcher & Wine to call-waiter only, since guests there expect full service.
4. **Commission angle:** Donna is on Uber Eats, which takes a commission on every delivery order. Sora takes none on in-venue orders. Don't claim Sora replaces delivery.
5. **Staff knowledge:** a review said a waiter didn't know the dishes. Sora menus can show descriptions on the guest's own phone.
6. **Franchise angle (Kung Thai):** if it works for them, it's a path to other Kung Thai branches. Ask, don't assume.

## Unknown (ask in the meeting)

- Which POS they use, and whether it's the same across all four venues.
- Covers per night, staff per shift, their busiest nights, how bar tabs work at Saint Yves.
- Who else decides (investors or the Kung Thai franchisor).

## Best research you can still do

Go to Saint Yves and Donna on a Friday night before the meeting. Watch how long ordering, drinks and the bill take, and mention what you saw. Nothing online beats that.

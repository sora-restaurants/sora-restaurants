Questions restaurants ask, and what to say

Each answer says what Sora does today. "Not yet" means it isn't built: never promise it as available.

Internet, power and going offline

What happens if our Wi-Fi goes down? Today

Guests order on their own phones, so they can switch to mobile data and carry on. The kitchen and bar screens need a connection: if a screen drops off, it reconnects by itself and reloads every open order, so nothing already sent is lost.

"Guests order on their own data, and the kitchen screen reconnects by itself and reloads the open orders. Keep a phone hotspot handy for the kitchen tablet as a backup."

Does Sora work fully offline? Not yet

No. Orders travel over the internet, so with no signal at all, nothing new can be sent. Staff take orders by hand, the way they do today, until the connection is back.

"It needs a connection to send orders. If everything is down, your waiters take orders the normal way. Nothing about your current setup goes away."

What about load-shedding? Today

Phones and tablets run on battery. If the router loses power, guests use mobile data, and the kitchen tablet needs its own signal: a phone hotspot or a data SIM. A small UPS on the router also keeps the Wi-Fi up.

"Your devices run on battery. Put the router on a UPS or give the kitchen tablet its own data, and Sora keeps going through load-shedding."

What if the kitchen tablet breaks or goes flat? Today

Orders are stored online, not on the tablet. Sign in to the kitchen screen on any other phone, tablet or laptop, and every open order is there.

"The orders live online, not on the tablet. Open the kitchen screen on any other device and you carry on."

What if a guest's order doesn't go through, or they tap Send twice? Today

Every order carries a unique key, so a double tap or a retry after a dropped connection gives one ticket, never two. If it really didn't send, the guest's phone says so and they can try again.

"Tapping twice still gives one ticket. We built it so the kitchen never gets duplicates."

What if Sora itself goes down? Today

Sora runs in the cloud, not on your premises. If it were ever unavailable, new QR orders can't be placed and staff take orders by hand until it's back. Orders already sent are saved.

"It runs in the cloud. If it ever has a problem, you're back to how you work today, and orders already placed are safe."

Paying

How do guests pay? Today

Each venue chooses: pay after the meal, pay when ordering, or let the guest choose. Pay after the meal works today: guests pay your normal way (card machine, till, cash), and Sora shows the waiter which tables still owe, so they can tap "Mark paid".

"Today your staff keep using your own card machines. Sora tracks which tables have paid, so none slip out."

Can guests pay by card in the app, or with Apple Pay or Google Pay? Not yet

Not yet. Pay-when-ordering is built into the flow, but no payment provider is connected, so it doesn't take real money. That needs a merchant account with a provider such as Yoco, PayFast or Peach Payments.

"In-app payment is our next step, once a payment provider is connected. Until then guests pay the normal way."

Does Sora touch our money or take a cut? Today

No commission on orders. In pay-after mode, Sora never handles the money at all.

Can guests split the bill, or add a tip? Not yet

Neither is built. Guests split and tip at the card machine, the way they do now.

Your POS, set-up and devices

Does it work with our POS? Today Not yet

It runs next to your POS; you don't replace anything. A direct link that sends Sora orders into your POS is planned, not built. Ask which POS they use: that decides which link gets built first.

"You keep your POS. Sora runs alongside it today. A direct link is planned, so tell me which POS you use."

Does it print kitchen tickets? Not yet

Orders show on a screen today. Sora already keeps a print queue, but the link to a kitchen printer isn't built.

What hardware do we need? Today

Nothing special: a tablet, phone or laptop for each kitchen or bar screen, a waiter phone if you want waiter calls, and the QR codes on the tables. Guests use their own phones.

Who sets it up, and how long does it take? Today Decide

We do: we load the menu, print and place the QR codes, set up the screens and train the staff on site. Agree a realistic timeframe with them before promising a date.

Guests and staff

Will it replace our waiters? Today

No. Waiters still serve every table. Sora takes the running back and forth off them, so they can look after guests.

"It's there to help your waiters, not replace them."

What about older guests, or anyone who wants a waiter? Today

The QR is optional. Anyone can still order from a waiter.

Do guests need to download an app? Today

No. The QR opens the menu in the phone's browser.

Can a guest call a waiter? Today

Yes. One tap sends the table number to the waiter screen, and it stays there until someone answers it.

Can guests see where their order is? Today

Yes. Their phone shows it moving through the kitchen, from sent to ready.

Is the menu in other languages? Not yet

Not today.

Is there a loyalty or rewards programme? Not yet

Not built.

Menu and running the venue

Can we change the menu ourselves? Today

Yes. Staff edit items and prices, and flip a dish to sold out, and it greys out on guests' phones straight away.

We have several venues. Does that work? Today

Yes. Each venue gets its own menu, QR codes, kitchen screen and dashboard, and staff who cover more than one kitchen can switch between them.

What do we see as owners? Today

A dashboard of QR orders: how many, their value, and when the busy hours were. It counts Sora orders only, not everything through your POS.

Can staff see another restaurant's orders? Today

No. Each restaurant's staff only see their own orders. It's built into the database and tested.

Data and privacy

Where is our data kept, and is it safe? Today

Sora runs on Vercel and Supabase (cloud providers), which may process data outside South Africa. Staff sign in with their own accounts and only see their own restaurant.

Do you collect guests' personal details? Today

Guests order without an account. If a guest asks for an emailed copy of the order, the email address is used only for that and deleted after 30 days.

Price and agreement

What does it cost? Decide

Each venue is quoted by its size: more tables means more QR codes to set up and maintain. Founding restaurants get a discounted introductory price. Have a rough range ready; give the exact quote once you've counted their tables.

"It depends on the size of the venue. I'll count your tables and give you an exact quote per venue, at the founding discount, with no commission on orders."

Is there a contract? Can we cancel? Decide

Decide your terms before the meeting: how long the founding period runs, the notice period, and what happens to the price afterwards. Don't make terms up on the spot.

Who else uses Sora? Today

No restaurants are live yet. That's why founding places are discounted, and why founding restaurants help shape what gets built.

"You'd be one of our first. You get the discount and a direct line to us."

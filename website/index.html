Questions owners asked us

Not answered here? Put it in your application and a founder will reply.

Send an application

Will Sora work with my restaurant's POS?

Yes, alongside it. Your POS stays untouched. Direct POS links are planned, and the POS you name on the form tells us which to build first.

What if the restaurant Wi-Fi drops?

Guests can order on mobile data. A kitchen screen that loses its connection reconnects by itself and reloads every open order.

My older guests want a waiter

They get one. Waiters still serve every table and the QR code is optional.

Do guests need an app to order?

No. The code opens the menu in the phone's browser.

Does table ordering work in load-shedding?

Yes, for as long as there is a connection. Phones and tablets run on batteries, and the kitchen tablet needs a signal of its own if your router goes down.

What does table ordering cost in the pilot?

Pilot pricing is set on application and is free or discounted for founding restaurants. Sora takes no commission on orders.
